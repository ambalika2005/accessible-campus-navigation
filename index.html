Project scope

Agent: route-planning assistant. Environment: synthetic campus graph. Inputs: start, destination, mobility preference, lift status and one closure. Outputs: candidate routes, costs, expanded nodes, support allocation and rule conclusions.

SDG connection: SDG 4 (inclusive education), SDG 10 (reduced inequalities), SDG 11 (inclusive and accessible communities).
